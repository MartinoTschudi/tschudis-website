Why / why not

For: no 04:30 alarm, no 3 hotels in 3 nights, 3 more villa days with friends, ~CHF 650 and 35k miles saved, 4.5 h flight once instead of 3 flights. Against: you lose Craveiral, the Costa Vicentina and the Lisbon evening — the design/food part of the trip. Gran Canaria's food scene is thinner; 14 nights on one island needs the excursions below to stay interesting.
